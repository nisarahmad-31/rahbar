| --- | --- |
| BS (Computer Science, Software Engineering, AI) | 10,000 |
| MS (All Engineering and Computing) | 12,500 |

## 2. Administrative Charges

| Charge | Amount (PKR) |
| --- | --- |
| Late registration fee | 3,000 |
| Official transcript per copy | 1,500 |
| Inter-campus transfer processing fee | 5,000 |

## 3. Tuition Refund Timeline

3.1 A full 100 percent tuition refund is granted when a course is dropped within the first 10 calendar days of classes. Between day 11 and day 20, a 50 percent refund applies. No tuition refund is issued after day 20.

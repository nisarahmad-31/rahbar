# University Grading Scale and GPA Policy

## 1. Letter Grades and Grade Points

1.1 The university uses a 4.00 grading scale: A+ and A carry 4.00 grade points, A- carries 3.67, B+ carries 3.33, B carries 3.00, C carries 2.00, D carries 1.00, and F carries 0.00 grade points.

## 2. Incomplete (I) Grades

2.1 A temporary Incomplete (I) grade may be awarded only when a student misses the final examination due to an approved emergency.

2.2 If an Incomplete grade is not resolved within the first two weeks of the following regular semester, it automatically converts to an F grade.

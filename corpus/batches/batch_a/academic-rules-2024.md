# Academic Rules Handbook (2024 Edition)

## 1. Academic Standing and Warning

1.1 An undergraduate student whose cumulative grade point average (CGPA) falls below 2.00 at the end of any regular semester is placed on Academic Warning.

1.2 A student who remains on Academic Warning for three consecutive regular semesters is dropped from the degree programme.

## 2. Course Repetition

2.1 A student may repeat a course in which the earned grade is C- or lower to improve the CGPA.

2.2 Only the grade of the latest attempt is counted in the CGPA calculation, even if it is lower than the previous grade.

## 3. Degree Completion

3.1 A minimum CGPA of 2.00 and completion of all credit hours specified in the curriculum are required for graduation.

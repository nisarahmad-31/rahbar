# Attendance and Examination Eligibility Policy

## 1. Minimum Attendance Requirement

1.1 A student must attend at least 80 percent of the scheduled lectures and laboratory sessions of a course to be eligible to sit the final examination.

1.2 A student who fails to meet the 80 percent attendance threshold is awarded an FA grade in that course.

## 2. Condonation for Medical Emergencies

2.1 The Head of Department may condone an attendance shortage of up to 10 percent strictly for documented inpatient medical emergencies submitted within five working days of resuming classes.

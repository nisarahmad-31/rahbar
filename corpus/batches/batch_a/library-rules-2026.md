# Central Library Borrowing and Conduct Rules

## 1. Borrowing Quotas and Loan Duration

1.1 Undergraduate students may borrow up to 5 physical books at a time for a loan period of 14 calendar days.

1.2 Graduate (MS and PhD) students may borrow up to 8 books at a time for a loan period of 21 calendar days.

## 2. Overdue Fines and Reference Material

2.1 Overdue books incur a late fine of PKR 50 per day per book. Reference textbooks and past theses may only be consulted inside the reading room.

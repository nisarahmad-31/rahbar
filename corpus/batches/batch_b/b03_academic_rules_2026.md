# Academic Rules Handbook (Fall 2026 Edition)

## 1. Academic Standing and Warning

1.1 An undergraduate student whose cumulative grade point average (CGPA) falls below 2.00 at the end of any regular semester is placed on Academic Warning.

1.2 Under the Fall 2026 revision, a student who remains on Academic Warning for two consecutive regular semesters and has a CGPA below 1.75 is dropped from the degree programme.

## 2. Course Repetition

2.1 A student may repeat any course with a grade below B to improve the CGPA, up to a maximum of four repeated courses across the degree.

2.2 Only the grade of the latest attempt is counted in the CGPA calculation.

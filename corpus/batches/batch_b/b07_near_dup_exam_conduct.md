# Examination Conduct and Dishonesty Policy (Fall 2026 Draft)

## 1. Prohibited Items in Examination Halls

1.1 Mobile phones, smartwatches, programmable calculators, and unauthorized written notes are strictly prohibited inside examination rooms during all assessments.

## 2. Penalties for Unfair Means

2.1 Any student found using unfair means during a mid-term or final examination shall have their paper cancelled and be referred to the Unfair Means Committee, which may award an F grade in the course or suspend enrollment for one full semester.

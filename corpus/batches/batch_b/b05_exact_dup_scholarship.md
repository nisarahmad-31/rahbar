# Merit and Need-Based Scholarship Policy (2026)

## 1. Dean's List Merit Scholarship

1.1 Full-time undergraduate students achieving a semester GPA of 3.75 or higher with at least 15 credit hours are eligible for a 50 percent tuition waiver for the following regular semester.

## 2. Need-Based Financial Aid

2.1 Students facing verified financial hardship may apply through the Financial Aid Office before the third week of classes with supporting income documentation.

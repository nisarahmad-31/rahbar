# University Fee Schedule and Refund Policy (2023)

## 1. Legacy Tuition Fee per Credit Hour

1.1 Under the 2023 fee schedule, undergraduate tuition was PKR 8,500 per credit hour and graduate tuition was PKR 10,500 per credit hour.

## 2. Legacy Refund Timeline

2.1 A 100 percent refund applied within the first 7 days of classes and 50 percent up to day 14.
